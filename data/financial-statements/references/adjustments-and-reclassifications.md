# Period-end adjustments and reclassifications

Use this as a checklist of what to look for, not as entries to book. For each item that fits the user's data, say what in the data suggests it (for example "no depreciation expense line: check the fixed asset schedule") and what the user should confirm with their accountant. Give no amounts the user didn't supply, and skip items that don't apply. Reply with the checklist, ordered with the most likely items first.

## Period-end adjustments

1. **Accruals:** expenses incurred but not yet paid (accounts payable accruals, payroll accruals, interest accruals).
2. **Deferrals:** prepaid expenses, deferred revenue and deferred costs adjusted for the period.
3. **Depreciation and amortization:** the period's charge from the fixed asset and intangible schedules.
4. **Bad debt provision:** the allowance for credit losses, based on the receivables aging and historical loss rates.
5. **Inventory adjustments:** write-downs for obsolete, slow-moving or impaired inventory.
6. **FX revaluation:** foreign-currency monetary assets and liabilities revalued at period-end rates.
7. **Tax provision:** current and deferred income tax expense.
8. **Fair value adjustments:** mark-to-market investments, derivatives and other fair-value items.

## Reclassifications

1. **Current and non-current:** long-term debt maturing within 12 months moves to current.
2. **Contra account netting:** allowances against gross receivables; accumulated depreciation against gross assets.
3. **Intercompany elimination:** intercompany balances and transactions are eliminated in consolidation.
4. **Discontinued operations:** their results move to a separate line item.
5. **Equity method adjustments:** the share of an investee's income or loss.
6. **Segment reclassifications:** transactions classified under the right operating segment.

## Signs a trial balance is unadjusted

- No depreciation or amortization expense, or accumulated depreciation that hasn't moved.
- No accrued expenses, payroll liabilities or tax payable.
- No income tax expense for a profitable period.
- Prepaid or deferred revenue balances that look the same as last period.

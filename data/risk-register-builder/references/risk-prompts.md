# Risk prompts by category

Use these to think of risks the user has not named. Pick only those that fit the project, and rewrite each with the project's own specifics (cause, event, consequence). Never copy a line as it stands.

## Schedule and delivery
- A key milestone depends on a dependency that has not confirmed availability.
- Team capacity drops through planned or unplanned absence in a critical period.
- Technical complexity is underestimated and estimates keep overrunning.
- An external approval (regulator, legal, procurement) takes longer than planned.

## Technical
- Integration with a third-party system is not yet prototyped or agreed.
- Existing technical debt makes the change harder or riskier than estimated.
- A security or compliance review needed before launch has not been scoped.
- Performance under production load is untested.
- Key technical knowledge sits with one person.

## Resource and people
- A key expert or engineer leaves or is unavailable in a critical phase.
- Budget for the next phase is not confirmed.
- The sponsor changes role or leaves.
- The team is not at full capacity (hiring lag, access, onboarding).

## Commercial and financial
- A vendor or partner contract is not yet signed.
- The cost estimate rests on unvalidated assumptions.
- The revenue or savings case depends on things outside the team's control.
- Currency exposure on international work.

## Compliance and regulatory
- A data privacy impact assessment is not complete.
- A regulatory approval is needed and its timeline is uncertain.
- A privacy or security standard that applies (GDPR, HIPAA, SOC 2, sector rules) is not yet mapped.
- Legal review of terms or contracts is pending.

## Stakeholder and adoption
- The main user group has low awareness or motivation to adopt.
- A team affected by the change resists it.
- The sponsor is not engaged and decisions are slow.
- The communications plan is not agreed with change management.

## External
- A market or competitor change undermines the business case.
- Economic conditions change budget or priority.
- A supplier or infrastructure provider fails or changes terms.
- The regulatory or geopolitical environment shifts.

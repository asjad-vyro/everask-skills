# Workstream item bank

Pick from these by depth and sector. Rewrite each as a specific request ("Last 3 years of monthly management accounts"). Items marked (T) suit tech-enabled targets, (V) are the vendor or partner variant.

## Financial (FIN)
- Last 3 years of audited or reviewed financial statements and latest monthly management accounts
- Quality of earnings: revenue and EBITDA adjustments, one-offs, owner add-backs
- Revenue breakdown by customer, product and period; recurring vs one-off split
- Working capital: monthly levels, normalised vs actual, seasonality
- Debt and debt-like items: loans, leases, deferred revenue, unpaid bonuses, tax liabilities
- Capex history: maintenance vs growth
- Tax returns, structure and open exposures
- Accounting policies, revenue recognition, audit adjustments and management letters
- Budget, forecast and the assumptions behind them; forecast vs actual history
- Related-party transactions and owner expenses run through the business
- Cash conversion, receivables ageing, payables ageing
- (V) Financial stability evidence: accounts, credit report, insurance

## Commercial (COM)
- Customer list with revenue share; concentration of the top 5 and top 10
- Retention, churn and cohort data; reasons customers left
- Customer contracts: term, renewal, termination and change-of-control clauses
- Pricing history, discounting, price increases and their effect
- Pipeline and backlog, with conversion history
- Market size, growth and competitors (source every figure)
- Customer references or calls (agree the list with the seller)
- Sales and marketing channels, cost of acquisition, effectiveness
- Dependence on one platform, marketplace or referral source

## Legal (LEG)
- Corporate documents: incorporation, cap table, shareholder agreements, board minutes
- Material contracts: customers, suppliers, partners, leases, with assignment and change-of-control terms
- Litigation: pending, threatened and settled in the last 5 years
- IP ownership: trademarks, patents, domains, and assignments from founders, employees and contractors
- Licences and permits needed to operate
- Regulatory compliance history and correspondence with regulators
- Employment contracts, non-competes, contractor classification
- Liens, guarantees and security over assets
- Data protection: policies, consents, breach history

## Operational (OPS)
- Management team: roles, tenure, background, who the business depends on
- Key person risk and succession plan
- Core processes and documentation; what only one person knows
- Supplier and vendor dependencies, single points of failure
- Facilities, leases and equipment condition
- Insurance policies, claims history, gaps
- Quality, delivery and customer complaint records
- Business continuity and incident history

## People (HR)
- Org chart, headcount history and open roles
- Compensation by role, bonus and equity schemes, benchmarks
- Benefits, pension and accrued leave obligations
- Retention risk for key employees; who would leave on a sale
- Attrition data and reasons, engagement surveys
- Disputes, grievances and disciplinary history
- Labour or union agreements where relevant
- Contractors and freelancers: terms and reliance

## Technology (TEC) (T)
- Architecture and stack overview; hosting and cloud costs
- Technical debt, code quality and test coverage
- Security posture: policies, penetration tests, certifications, incidents
- Open-source licences in use and third-party dependencies
- Ownership of code and who wrote it (employees, contractors, agencies)
- Product roadmap, R&D spend and team capacity
- Uptime, incident log and support metrics
- Backups, disaster recovery and access controls
- Data privacy compliance across the markets served

## Environmental, social and governance (ESG), where relevant
- Environmental liabilities, past or present contamination
- Environmental permits and compliance history
- Health and safety record
- Supply chain and labour practices
- Governance: board, controls, anti-bribery policies

## Sector add-ons
- Software and SaaS: ARR quality and definition, cohort retention, net revenue retention, hosting cost per customer, security certifications, open-source exposure
- Agency and services: client concentration, utilisation, project profitability, staff dependence, subcontractor reliance, contract terms and scope disputes
- E-commerce and consumer: marketplace and ad-platform dependence, gross margin by product, returns, inventory ageing, supplier terms, brand and channel mix, seasonality
- Healthcare: regulatory approvals, reimbursement and payor mix, clinical compliance
- Industrial and manufacturing: equipment condition, environmental remediation, safety record, supply chain
- Financial services: regulatory capital, compliance history, credit quality
- Real estate and hospitality: titles, leases, occupancy, maintenance backlog
- Vendor or partner check (V): financial stability, security and data handling, references, exit and data-return terms, subcontractors, insurance

## Typical red flags to adapt
- One customer or channel above a large share of revenue (set the level with the user's facts)
- Owner add-backs that are large relative to profit
- Founders or contractors who never signed IP assignments
- Revenue recognised ahead of delivery, or unexplained gaps between accounts and bank
- Key contracts that end or can be terminated on a change of control
- Seller slow or evasive on specific requests
- Staff who hold all the knowledge and have no retention terms
- Undisclosed disputes, regulator contact or breaches

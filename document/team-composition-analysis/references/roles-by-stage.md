# Roles by stage

Use this to compare a team with what is usual at its stage and to pick the next hires. These are commonly quoted shapes for software startups, not rules: the user's stage, product and goals come first.

## Who is usual at each stage

Engineering
- Pre-seed: the founders write the code; 0-1 contract developers.
- Seed: an engineering lead (first), 2-3 full-stack engineers, 1 frontend or backend specialist.
- Series A: VP Engineering, 2-3 senior engineers, 3-5 mid-level engineers, 1-2 junior engineers, 1 DevOps or infrastructure engineer.

Sales and marketing
- Pre-seed: the founders sell; contract marketing help.
- Seed: a first sales hire or head of sales, a marketing or growth lead, an SDR or BDR if the business is B2B.
- Series A: VP Sales, 3-5 account executives, 2-3 SDRs or BDRs, a marketing manager, a content or demand-gen hire.

Product and design
- Pre-seed: a founder is the product lead.
- Seed: a first product manager; a contract designer.
- Series A: Head of Product, 1-2 product managers, a product designer, a UX researcher (optional).

Customer success and support
- Pre-seed: the founders handle support.
- Seed: a first customer success hire (optional).
- Series A: a CS manager, 2-4 CS representatives, a technical support engineer.

G&A (finance, operations, people)
- Pre-seed: contractors for accounting and legal.
- Seed: an operations or office manager; a contract CFO.
- Series A: a CFO or finance lead, a recruiter, an office manager or executive assistant.

Department mix at Series A (commonly quoted): engineering about 40% (6-20 people), sales and marketing 30% (5-15), customer success 10% (2-5), G&A 10% (2-5), product 10% (2-5).

## Reporting lines

Pre-seed
```
Founders (flat)
├── Contractors
└── First hires (report to the founders)
```

Seed
```
CEO
├── Engineering Lead (2-4 engineers)
├── Sales or Growth Lead (1-2 reps)
├── Product Manager
└── Operations
```

Series A
```
CEO
├── CTO or VP Engineering (6-20 people)
│   ├── Engineering managers
│   └── Individual contributors
├── VP Sales (5-15 people)
│   ├── Sales manager
│   ├── Account executives
│   └── SDRs
├── Head of Product (2-5 people)
│   ├── Product managers
│   └── Designers
├── Head of Customer Success (2-5 people)
└── CFO or finance lead (2-5 people)
    ├── Recruiter
    └── Operations
```

## Span of control (commonly quoted)

- First-line managers: 4-8 direct reports.
- Directors: 3-5 direct reports (managers).
- VPs: 3-5 direct reports (directors).
- CEO: 5-8 direct reports (the executive team).

## Full-time or contract

Full-time for core product development, revenue-generating roles (sales), mission-critical operations and roles that hold institutional knowledge.

Contract for short-term specialist needs (legal, accounting), variable workload (design, marketing campaigns), skills outside the team's core, testing a role before a full-time hire, and opening a new region before a permanent presence.

- Full-time: lower hourly cost; benefits and overhead; a long-term commitment; cultural fit matters.
- Contract: higher hourly rate; no benefits or overhead; flexible, easy to scale up or down.

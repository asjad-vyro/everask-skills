# Pay, equity, timing and budget benchmarks

Commonly quoted US figures from about 2024. They are static, may be out of date, are not from a dataset this skill can name, and do not fit other countries. When you use one, label it ("US benchmark, about 2024" or "rule of thumb"), and prefer a figure you found by searching.

## Base salary (US, about 2024, USD a year)

Engineering
- Junior: 90K-120K
- Mid-level: 120K-150K
- Senior: 150K-180K
- Staff or principal: 180K-220K
- Engineering manager: 160K-200K
- VP Engineering: 180K-250K
- First engineering lead at seed: 150K-180K
- Full-stack engineer at seed: 120K-150K
- Frontend or backend specialist at seed: 130K-160K
- DevOps or infrastructure engineer: 140K-170K

## Location adjustments (US only, about 2024)

- San Francisco, New York: +20-30% above the ranges
- Seattle, Boston, Los Angeles: +10-20%
- Austin, Denver, Chicago: +0-10%
- Remote or other US cities: -10-20%
- Outside the US: varies widely by country. Search instead of adjusting.

## Fully loaded cost

Typical components: base salary; employer payroll taxes (7.65% FICA in the US); benefits such as health insurance and a retirement plan (commonly quoted at 10K-15K per employee a year); workspace, equipment and software (5K-10K per employee a year). The shortcut is base x 1.3 to 1.4, and the plan uses 1.3. If the user gives their real benefit costs, use those instead. Commission or bonus is added on top.

## Contractors

Commonly quoted: 75-200 USD an hour for a contractor, against 40-100 USD an hour for a full-time equivalent. Contractors carry no benefits or overhead.

## Equity (rules of thumb, percent of the company)

Founders: first founder 40-60%, second 20-40%, third 10-20%. Vesting over 4 years with a 1-year cliff is the common default. For two founders the ranges do not fit neatly (an even split sits outside the second-founder range): say so, and treat the split as the founders' decision, based on who contributes what.

Pre-seed employees: first engineer 0.5-2.0%; each of the first five employees 0.25-1.0%.

Seed hires: VP or head 0.5-1.5%; senior individual contributor 0.1-0.5%; mid-level 0.05-0.25%; junior 0.01-0.1%.

Series A hires: C-level (CTO, CFO) 1.0-3.0%; VP 0.3-1.0%; director 0.1-0.5%; senior individual contributor 0.05-0.2%; mid-level 0.01-0.1%; junior 0.005-0.05%.

## Option pool

Reserved or topped up by round: pre-seed 10-15%; seed 10-15% top-up; Series A 10-15% top-up; Series B and later 5-10% per round.

Investors often ask for the pool to be created before the round, so the dilution falls on the existing holders (usually the founders), not on the new investors. Illustration with made-up numbers: pre-money $10M plus $5M of new money is a $15M post-money. A 15% post-money pool is worth 15% x $15M = $2.25M. If the whole pool is new and sits in the pre-money, the existing holders' effective pre-money value is $10M - $2.25M = $7.75M.

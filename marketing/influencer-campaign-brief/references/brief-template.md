# Creator brief template

Fill every section. Keep the finished brief to one or two pages, one to three lines per section. Replace every angle-bracket prompt with the user's facts, `not given - add before sending`, or a labelled assumption. The delivered brief contains no angle brackets.

---

## <Brand> x <Creator> - <campaign name>

**Campaign goal:** <one plain sentence ending in the metric the user gave>
**Context:** <what is promoted, geography, campaign moment; skip for repeat creators>

### Why you
<what the user told you about this creator's work and audience, and which strength the campaign leans on. If nothing was given: "Brand to complete before sending.">

### Audience
<who the content is for, their current pain, the belief shift wanted; mark as assumption if not given>

### Key messages

**Prohibited claims and comparisons:** <list, with the approved alternative wording beside each where one exists>
**Mandatory wording (must appear exactly):** <verbatim offer or legal language, or "none">

- Offer: <price, discount, code, validity - exactly as the deal gives it>
- Disclosure: see the Disclosure section.
- <each other locked item, one line: prohibited topics, accuracy standard, brand-name spelling>

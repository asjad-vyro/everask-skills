# Review Platform Rules

Read this before you propose incentives, invitation groups, moderation requests or reuse of platform assets. Platform policies change, so the table below is a starting point, not the current rulebook. Before you tell the user what a platform allows, use `web_search` or `web_extract` to open the platform's current official guidelines, say what you found and link the page. If you cannot confirm a rule, say it is unconfirmed and propose the version with no incentive. A platform's permission is not a guarantee that local law, the reviewer's employer rules or the user's contract permit the same action.

## Collection and incentives

| Platform | Rule as last known | Start from |
|----------|--------------------|------------|
| Google Maps / Business Profile | No incentives for reviews or for changing or removing them. Do not selectively ask for positive reviews or discourage negative ones. Invite genuine customers neutrally. | Google's contributed content policy (Maps user-generated content) |
| Trustpilot | Fair, neutral invitations through supported methods. A neutral selection system cannot filter by expected sentiment. Incentives are restricted. | Trustpilot's guidelines for businesses |
| G2 | No biased collection or positive-only eligibility. Permitted nominal incentives must be disclosed to G2 and labeled. Follow the current campaign rules and cap. | G2's community guidelines |
| Capterra | Actual users write original reviews, with no AI-written or third-party-written reviews. Permitted incentive programs need disclosed terms and exclude certain relationships and roles. | Capterra's community guidelines |

Do not offer a universal gift-card amount. For a permitted G2 or Capterra campaign, verify the platform's process, budget, reviewer eligibility, disclosure wording, incentive cap and fulfillment rules before drafting an offer. The platform's moderation may decide whether a submission qualifies; the business must not judge eligibility by positivity. If the rules or the user's authorization are missing, give a non-incentivized request instead.

**Satisfaction-score gating is different from experience-based timing.** "After the first completed project" is a defined experience milestone. "Only users who rated us 9 or 10" selects expected positive feedback, and is not allowed. Keep support follow-up independent of who gets a public invitation. An opt-out or a genuinely ineligible reviewer can be excluded with a recorded, neutral reason.

## Moderation

Report a specific, documented rule breach through the platform's supported process. Bad ratings and disputed opinions are not automatically violations. Track pending appeals without claiming deletion is guaranteed.

For impersonation or exposure of sensitive data, preserve evidence in the user's private records and use the relevant platform category. Do not publish more private information in a rebuttal. Refuse requests to fabricate reviews, recruit fake identities or coordinate false reports, and offer genuine invitations and evidence-based moderation instead.

## Testimonial reuse

Check both the customer's permission and the platform's content and brand rights. G2 logos and trademarks need the appropriate permission, and Capterra has specific attribution, disclaimer and asset-use rules. Use approved platform assets or permissioned business-owned quotes where allowed. Keep incentive and material-connection disclosures with the reused asset. Do not imply a platform endorsement or a current badge without evidence and rights. If permission is missing, the proposed placement stays a draft, and the reviewer should not be contacted repeatedly.

## United States review rule

For US campaigns, the FTC's rule on consumer reviews and testimonials restricts fake reviews and incentives conditioned, expressly or implicitly, on a review's sentiment. An incentive disclosure does not cure a positivity condition. Selected reviews used in advertising become testimonials, with separate endorsement considerations. Use `web_search` to open the FTC's current guidance and link it. Apply the selected platform's stricter rules as well. This is not legal approval for a campaign.

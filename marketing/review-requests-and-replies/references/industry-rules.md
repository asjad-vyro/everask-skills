# Industry rules for review replies and requests

Read the section for the user's industry before writing replies. This is general guidance, not legal advice; the user should check their own regulator's rules.

## Healthcare (medical, dental, chiropractic, therapy)

- Never confirm or deny that the reviewer is or was a patient, even if they say so themselves.
- Never mention diagnoses, treatments, medications, appointments or any health detail, even if the reviewer did first.
- Safe pattern for any negative review: "Thank you for your feedback. We take all patient experiences seriously. Please contact our office at [phone] to discuss your concerns."
- Positive replies stay generic: "Thank you for the kind words. Our whole team appreciates it."
- Anyone on staff who replies to reviews should know these rules. In the US this relates to HIPAA; elsewhere, similar privacy rules apply.
- Platforms people check: Healthgrades, Vitals, ZocDoc, WebMD, RateMDs, Google.

## Legal

- Never confirm someone is or was a client. Never mention case details, outcomes or strategy.
- Check the state or national bar's advertising and testimonial rules before reusing reviews in marketing.
- Platforms: Avvo, Martindale-Hubbell, Lawyers.com, Google.

## Home services (HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, remodeling)

- Encourage customers to add photos of finished work to their review.
- Replies may mention the type of job in general terms, not the customer's address or private details: "That kitchen remodel was a great project, glad you love the tile work."
- Platforms: Angi, Thumbtack, Houzz, BBB, Google.

## Restaurants and hospitality

- Yelp and TripAdvisor matter most here, alongside Google. Delivery app ratings also affect discovery.
- Do not ask for Yelp reviews directly. Do not offer free food or discounts for reviews.
- For a food-safety or illness complaint, do not argue; apologise, take it offline and tell the user to follow it up internally.

## Automotive

- Platforms: DealerRater, Cars.com, Edmunds, CarGurus, Google.

## Real estate

- Platforms: Zillow, Realtor.com, Homes.com, Google. Do not reveal deal details (price, address, parties) in a reply.

## Financial services

- Do not mention accounts, balances, products held or advice given. Platforms: NerdWallet, Bankrate, Google. Check the regulator's rules on testimonials.
